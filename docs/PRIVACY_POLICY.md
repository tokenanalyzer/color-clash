## Summary

War of Love is a single-player, offline match‑3 puzzle game. **It does not
collect, store, or transmit any personal or device data.** It has no user
accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third‑party SDKs, and requests no
internet permission.

## Information we collect

**None.** War of Love does not collect, process, or share any personal
information, usage data, device identifiers, location, contacts, or any
other data.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress — levels completed, coins, boosters, and in‑game settings
— is saved **locally on your device only**, in the app's private storage. It
is never uploaded, synced, or shared. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions

The app requests a single Android permission:

- **`VIBRATE`** — used only for local haptic feedback during gameplay. It
  accesses no data and requires no network.

The app does **not** request the `INTERNET` permission and has no network
capability.

## Third‑party services

War of Love integrates **no** third‑party services: no analytics (e.g.
Firebase/Google Analytics), no advertising networks (e.g. AdMob), no crash
reporting, no social SDKs, and no in‑app‑purchase billing.
