## Summary

War of Love is a single-player match-3 puzzle game. The game itself has no
user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social features, no
cloud save, and no in-app purchases. Your game progress is stored **only on
your device**.

The app shows advertising through **Google AdMob** (the Google Mobile Ads
SDK). To request and display ads, the app needs an internet connection and
the AdMob SDK — a Google service — collects a limited set of device data as
described below. **The game is fully playable without ads**: watching an ad
is always optional, ads only appear at natural break points (never during a
puzzle), and if the device is offline or an ad fails the game continues
normally.

## Information the app itself collects

**None.** War of Love has no servers of its own. It does not collect an
email, name, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or any
account information, and it does not send your game data anywhere.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress — levels completed, coins, boosters, and in-game
settings — is saved **locally on your device only**, in the app's private
storage. It is never uploaded, synced, or shared by the game. Uninstalling
the app removes it.

## Advertising — Google AdMob

The app uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads (which you choose to watch in
exchange for an in-game bonus) and occasional full-screen ads between
levels. To do this, the **Google Mobile Ads SDK** may collect and process:

- the device **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier) and similar
  device identifiers;
- IP address, and coarse (approximate) location derived from it;
- device information such as model, operating-system version, language, and
  basic network state;
- ad interaction data (that an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked).

This data is handled by Google as an independent service, under
**Google's Privacy Policy** (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and the
**Google Mobile Ads / AdMob** terms
(<https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>). Google uses it to serve
and measure ads and, subject to your device settings and regional consent
requirements, to personalize ads. You can limit ad personalization and reset
or delete your Advertising ID from your device settings
(*Settings → Privacy → Ads* on Android).

The app does **not** combine this with any data of its own, does not build
its own advertising profile, and has no other advertising, analytics, or
tracking SDK.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | request and display AdMob ads |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | let the Ads SDK check connectivity before requesting an ad |
| `VIBRATE` | local haptic feedback during gameplay (accesses no data) |
| `AD_ID` (declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+) | access the device Advertising ID for ad serving |

The app requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or
phone permissions.

## Your choices

- **Play without ads:** simply don't tap the "watch an ad" buttons; the
  full-screen ads between levels are infrequent and capped.
- **Limit ad tracking:** reset or delete your Advertising ID and turn on
  "opt out of Ads personalization" in your device settings.
- **Regional rights:** where applicable (e.g. GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA), you
  may have rights over data processed by Google as described in Google's
  Privacy Policy; contact us at the address below with questions about this
  app.

## Data Safety form (Google Play) — summary of what to declare

- **Device or other IDs:** collected (Advertising ID), shared with Google
  for advertising. Not user-deletable by the app; resettable via device
  settings.
- **Location — approximate:** collected by the Ads SDK (from IP) for
  advertising.
- **App activity (ad interactions):** collected for advertising and
  measurement.
- Data is **not** encrypted in transit by the app itself only in the sense
  that all transfer is performed by the Google SDK over HTTPS; the app adds
  no plaintext transfer.
- No data collected by the game itself; no account data; no data sold.

Confirm the exact declarations against Google's current AdMob "Data
disclosure" guidance before submitting.
